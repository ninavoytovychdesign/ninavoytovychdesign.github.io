Context

The only designer on a live investment platform

I own the product end to end: flows, interface, the design system and handoff to development, working directly with stakeholders. The scope started with investment journeys and grew into a multi-product architecture.

Problem

Seven steps before the first investment

The investor questionnaire had seven steps, and not all of them were needed to start investing.

Decision

Required steps stay, optional ones wait

I kept the steps needed to start in onboarding and moved the optional ones into account settings, where an investor can fill them in later.
